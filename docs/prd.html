Product Requirements Document

Formal requirements specification for the UNIUTY University Portal — a role-based, biometric-secured academic management system serving students, lecturers, and administrators across all 14 departments.

Document Version: 1.0.0  |  Status: Approved  |  Last Updated: June 2026

1. Problem Statement

UNIUTY currently relies on fragmented, largely paper-based systems to manage the full student lifecycle — from admission through graduation. This creates compounding inefficiencies across every academic function:

1.1 Core Pain Points

DomainCurrent StateImpactSeverity
Result Management Lecturers submit paper score sheets; registry staff manually transcribe into spreadsheets 6–10 week result processing cycle; transcription errors averaging 3.2% per batch Critical
Identity Verification Student ID cards are the sole verification method; cards are easily forged or borrowed Estimated 8–12 exam impersonation cases per semester go undetected Critical
Attendance Tracking Paper sign-in sheets; no real-time visibility; easy to forge proxy signatures Attendance data unreliable; cannot enforce 75% attendance policy fairly High
Fee Payment Bank teller slips presented to bursary; manual reconciliation weekly Delays in clearance; ghost payments; 2–4 week reconciliation lag High
Data Fragmentation Student data spread across registry, bursary, departments, and hostel systems with no sync Duplicate records; contradictory data; re-entry errors; no single source of truth High
Reporting & Oversight Vice Chancellor's office relies on manually compiled Excel reports from each department Reports are stale by the time they arrive; no drill-down capability Medium
Admission Management Admission decisions communicated via notice boards and physical letters Qualified candidates miss deadlines; fraud in admission lists Medium

1.2 Problem Scope

The proposed UNIUTY Portal addresses all seven pain points above through a unified, web-based platform with hardware biometric integration. The system does not seek to replace the university's existing accounting (SAGE) or library (Koha) systems in this phase; integration hooks will be provided via REST API.

3. User Goals

3.1 Student Goals

  • View semester results immediately after publication without visiting the registry
  • Download official result transcripts and fee receipts as PDFs at any time
  • Check attendance status per course in real time to avoid disqualification
  • Pay fees online without visiting the bank or bursary queue
  • Receive instant notifications when results are published or fees are due
  • Access the portal from any device without installing a native app

3.2 Lecturer Goals

  • Submit continuous assessment and exam scores directly from a spreadsheet-like interface
  • Verify student identity via biometric before allowing exam entry
  • Record attendance quickly via NFC tap or facial scan — no paper sheets
  • See a live class list with photos to identify students
  • Edit submitted scores within an approved window before final ratification
  • Generate attendance reports for Senate board meetings

3.3 Administrator Goals

  • Manage the full user lifecycle: onboard, assign roles, suspend, and deactivate accounts
  • Approve and ratify result batches submitted by lecturers before they go live to students
  • Manage departmental data: courses, staff assignments, timetables
  • Generate financial reconciliation reports comparing payments vs. fees owed
  • Monitor biometric enrollment coverage to ensure full compliance
  • Configure system-wide settings: academic calendar, fee schedules, grace periods

3.4 Vice Chancellor Goals

  • Access a real-time executive dashboard with university-wide KPIs
  • Drill down into departmental performance, pass rates, and attendance trends
  • Review and approve promotion of staff to administrator-level roles
  • Access audit logs for compliance and governance reporting
  • Receive automated weekly digest reports via email
